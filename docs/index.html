How it works

A scheduled job sweeps every retailer for all products above. Products marked 🔔 alerts push an urgent notification to the phone via ntfy the moment they become buyable; products marked 🛒 auto-buy are additionally handed to the (off-by-default) checkout automation. Toggle either per product in config.yml. Status here refreshes when the site redeploys; the pushes are instant.
